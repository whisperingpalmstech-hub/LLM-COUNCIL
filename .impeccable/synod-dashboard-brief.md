# Synod Dashboard Surface Brief

- Scope: primary dashboard, session workspace, drawers, interview modal, and setup modal.
- Mode: Operate.
- Audience/job: founders, operators, consultants, and leadership teams framing, running, and reviewing consequential decisions.
- Primary task: enter a decision, monitor the council, and reach an accountable verdict without losing orientation.
- Content/proof: real presenter video, live stage state, role activity, structured verdict, execution plan, history, councils, settings, and provider health.
- Constraints: preserve all current IDs and behaviors; presenter remains prominent; no raw JSON; responsive and keyboard accessible; glass must preserve contrast.
- Direction: Mission Control Veil / Flight-Path Workspace.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/mission-control-flightpath.png`.
- Memorable moment: one luminous route connects the decision prompt to Interview, Deliberate, Decide, and Execute beneath the council chair.
- Responsive rule: desktop uses asymmetric rail/video/workspace topology; mobile linearizes to presenter, prompt, route, then live council without horizontal overflow.
