# Synod Council

## Decision intelligence for the moments that matter

Important decisions rarely fail because people lack ideas. They fail because assumptions stay invisible, risks arrive late, and discussion never becomes an accountable next step.

Synod Council turns a question into a structured decision brief: a recommendation, its rationale, the risks worth respecting, the actions to take, and the evidence still missing.

## From question to decision you can defend

Ask Synod for a **Quick answer** when speed matters. Switch to **Detailed advice** when the stakes rise. Synod assembles the relevant perspectives, challenges weak logic, and brings the result back as one readable answer—not a pile of raw model output.

### What teams get

- Clear recommendations and practical next actions
- Visible risks, alternatives, assumptions, and unanswered questions
- A dynamic council that scales with the complexity of the choice
- Optional guided interviews when context genuinely matters
- Auditable transcripts and decision history
- Local Obsidian-compatible decision memory with per-session consent
- File, audio, image, GitHub repository, and phone-based context intake
- Real provider health, resilience notices, and controlled fallback behavior

## A better operating rhythm

1. Frame the choice with outcome, constraints, and timing.
2. Choose Quick answer or Detailed advice.
3. Add context from a file, GitHub repository, microphone, or phone.
4. Watch a visible council process without parsing internal payloads.
5. Act from a recommendation, risk view, success measures, and next step.

## Designed for trust, not theatre

- Raw JSON stays out of the primary experience.
- Supporting perspectives are available without overwhelming the result.
- Provider fallbacks are disclosed rather than hidden.
- Active context makes the persona, outcome, and memory consent visible.
- Credentials remain masked in Settings.
